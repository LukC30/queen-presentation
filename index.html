The Four Band Members

Four distinct songwriters, one unmatched chemistry

Freddie Mercury
Lead vocals & piano. Legendary 4-octave voice range and unforgettable stage showmanship.
Brian May
Guitarist & PhD astrophysicist. Handcrafted his world-famous guitar "Red Special" with his father.
Roger Taylor
Drums & backing vocals. High-energy rhythm with high-pitched harmonies that defined Queen's sound.
John Deacon
Bass guitar. The reserved musical genius who composed mega-hits like Another One Bites the Dust.

★ Unbelievable Fact: All four members wrote at least one #1 worldwide hit song!

1975 Masterpiece

Bohemian Rhapsody

The impossible song that broke all radio rules

  • Nearly 6 Minutes Long: Record labels strongly said: "Radios will never play this!"
  • Rock Meets Opera: Fused heavy metal guitar riffs with delicate Italian classical opera.
  • No Chorus: Flows naturally through ballad, opera, and hard rock sections.
  • Global Phenomenon: Over 2 billion streams on Spotify alone today.
