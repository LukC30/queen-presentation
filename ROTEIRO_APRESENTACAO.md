# 🎙️ Queen Presentation: 10-Minute Oral Script (A2+/B1)
**Speaker:** Lu  
**Topic:** Queen: The Timeless Legends of Rock  
**Target Duration:** ~10 minutes (Pacing: ~1m15s to 1m30s per slide)

---

## 📌 Dicas Práticas para a Hora da Apresentação
- **Não decore palavra por palavra:** Olhe para o slide, lembre-se da ideia e fale com as suas palavras.
- **Fale com calma:** Se você falar devagar, seu inglês soa mais claro e você preenche o tempo sem ansiedade.
- **Respire entre as frases:** Pausas de 2 a 3 segundos são super naturais e mostram tranquilidade.

## 🟡 Slide 1: Introduction (~1 min)
**Visual:** Cover slide with *QUEEN.* and your name.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Good morning, everyone! Today, I’m really excited to talk about one of my favorite bands of all time: **Queen**.
>
> I chose Queen because their music is truly timeless. It doesn’t matter how old you are, or what kind of music you usually listen to—I am 100% sure that everyone in this room has heard at least two or three Queen songs.
>
> In this presentation, I want to show you who they were, their biggest achievements, their special connection with Brazil, and why their songs are still everywhere today. Let’s get started!"

---

## 🟡 Slide 2: The Band Members (~1m30s)
**Visual:** The 4 cards with each member.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Queen was formed in London in 1970. What made Queen so unique was that every single member was an extraordinary musician and songwriter:
>
> - First, **Freddie Mercury**, the lead singer and pianist. He had an incredible 4-octave voice range and a dramatic, theatrical presence on stage.
> - Next, **Brian May**, the guitarist. He is actually a PhD astrophysicist! And he built his famous electric guitar, the 'Red Special', by hand with his father.
> - Then, **Roger Taylor**, the drummer. He had high-energy rock vocals with high-pitched harmonies that you hear in almost every Queen chorus.
> - And finally, **John Deacon**, the quiet bass player. Even though he was very shy, he wrote some of their most famous danceable hits like *Another One Bites the Dust*.
>
> In fact, here is an amazing fact: **all four members wrote at least one worldwide number-one hit song!**"

---

## 🟡 Slide 3: Bohemian Rhapsody (~1m30s)
**Visual:** Bullet points + Video embed.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Now, let's talk about their biggest masterpiece: **Bohemian Rhapsody**, released in 1975.
>
> When the band finished recording the track, the record company bosses hated the idea of releasing it. They said: *'Are you crazy? It’s almost six minutes long! Radio stations will never play it!'*
>
> But Freddie and the band refused to change it. The song has no traditional chorus, and it boldly combines three different worlds: a soft piano ballad, an Italian opera section, and heavy rock guitars.
>
> Today, it is considered one of the greatest songs in human history, with over two billion streams on Spotify alone!"

---

## 🟡 Slide 4: Live Aid 1985 (~1m30s)
**Visual:** Live Aid stats & video clip.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "In July 1985, Queen gave the most legendary performance of their lives at **Live Aid** in London.
>
> Live Aid was a massive charity concert to raise money for Africa. There were 72,000 people in Wembley Stadium, and nearly two billion people watching live on television worldwide.
>
> Queen only had **20 minutes** on stage. But Freddie Mercury completely dominated the entire stadium. He did his famous vocal call—the *'Ay-Oh!'* call-and-response—and every single person in the crowd repeated it with him.
>
> Music critics around the world still vote those 20 minutes as the **greatest live rock performance of all time**."

---

## 🟡 Slide 5: Iconic Albums & Diversity (~1m30s)
**Visual:** 4 album covers (Opera, News, Jazz, The Game).

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Another thing I love about Queen is that they never stayed stuck in just one genre. 
>
> If you look at these four albums:
> - In **A Night at the Opera**, they explored operatic rock and acoustic ballads.
> - In **News of the World**, they created *We Will Rock You* and *We Are The Champions*—which became the biggest sports anthems in the world.
> - In **Jazz**, they released *Don’t Stop Me Now*, which scientists proved to be one of the happiest songs ever written because of its energetic rhythm.
> - And in **The Game**, they experimented with funk and disco with *Another One Bites the Dust*.
>
> They proved that a rock band has no limits."

---

## 🟡 Slide 6: Queen in Brazil (Rock in Rio 1985) (~1m30s)
**Visual:** 3 cards (250,000 People, Love of My Life, Emotional Impact).

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Now, a chapter that is very close to our hearts: **Queen’s special connection with Brazil!**
>
> In January 1985, Queen came to Rio de Janeiro for the very first edition of **Rock in Rio**. 
>
> They performed for over **250,000 people** in a single night—this was the largest concert crowd in Queen's entire career!
>
> During the song *Love of My Life*, something magical happened. The entire Brazilian crowd started singing the lyrics in perfect English, word for word, louder than the sound system. Freddie was so shocked and moved that he stopped singing and started conducting the crowd like an orchestra.
>
> He later said that this was one of the most emotional moments of his entire life."

---

## 🟡 Slide 7: Queen in Pop Culture (~1m30s)
**Visual:** 3 cards (Cinema, Gaming World, Anime References).

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Queen didn't stay in the 70s and 80s—their music is still alive in everything we consume today:
>
> - **In Cinema:** The 2018 movie *Bohemian Rhapsody* was a massive success, making over 900 million dollars and winning four Oscars. It introduced Queen to an entire new generation of teenagers.
> - **In Video Games:** You can play and listen to their music in games like *Guitar Hero*, *Grand Theft Auto V*, *Fortnite Festival*, and *Just Dance*.
> - **And in Anime:** If you watch anime, you might know *JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure*. The author, Hirohiko Araki, is a huge Queen fan, and he named major stands directly after their songs—like *Killer Queen*, *Sheer Heart Attack*, and *Bites the Dust*!
>
> Queen is literally everywhere."

>
> As Freddie always said: *'The show must go on.'*
>
> For me, Queen represents the power of being bold, authentic, and having fun with what you create.
