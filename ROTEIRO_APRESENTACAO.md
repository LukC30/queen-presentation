# 🎙️ Queen Presentation: 10-Minute Oral Script (A2+/B1)
**Speaker:** Lu  
**Topic:** Queen: The Timeless Legends of Rock  
**Target Duration:** ~9 to 10 minutes (pacing: ~1.5 min per slide)

---

## 📌 General Delivery Tips
- **Speak slowly:** Em inglês, falar devagar não é defeito, transmite autoridade e ajuda a gastar os 10 minutos sem correr.
- **Eye contact:** Olhe para o professor e para os colegas, não fique lendo o slide.
- **Slide transitions:** Use frases de transição para conectar as ideias suavemente.

---

## 🟡 Slide 1: Introduction (~1m30s)
**Visual:** Cover slide with *QUEEN.* and your name.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Good morning, everyone! Today, I’m really excited to talk about one of my favorite bands of all time: **Queen**.
>
> I chose Queen because their music is truly timeless. It doesn’t matter how old you are, or what kind of music you usually listen to—I am 100% sure that everyone in this room has heard at least two or three Queen songs. 
>
> Their songs are in movies, commercials, and sports events all over the world.
>
> In this presentation, I want to show you who they were, how they created their biggest masterpieces, and why they are still so influential today. So, let’s begin!"

---

## 🟡 Slide 2: The Band Members (~1m45s)
**Visual:** The 4 cards with each member.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Queen was formed in London in 1970. What made Queen so special was that it wasn't just 'Freddie Mercury and backup musicians'—all four members were incredible artists and songwriters:
>
> - First, **Freddie Mercury**, the lead singer and pianist. He had an astonishing vocal range of almost four octaves, but what really set him apart was his theatrical energy on stage.
> - Next, **Brian May**, the guitarist. Believe it or not, Brian is actually a PhD astrophysicist! And he built his famous electric guitar, called the 'Red Special', by hand with his father using wood from an old fireplace.
> - Then we have **Roger Taylor**, the drummer. He had a powerful rock voice with high-pitched harmonies that you can hear in almost every Queen chorus.
> - And finally, **John Deacon**, the quiet bass player. Even though he was very shy, he wrote some of their most danceable and funky global hits.
>
> In fact, here is a cool fun fact: **every single member of Queen wrote at least one number-one worldwide hit**. That is extremely rare in rock history!"

---

## 🟡 Slide 3: Bohemian Rhapsody (~1m45s)
**Visual:** The bullet points + Video embed.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Now, let's talk about the song that changed rock music forever: **Bohemian Rhapsody**, released in 1975.
>
> When the band finished recording it, the record company executives hated the idea of releasing it as a single. They said: *'Are you crazy? It’s almost six minutes long! Radios will never play a song this long!'*
>
> But Freddie and the band refused to cut it. The song has no traditional chorus, and it boldly mixes three totally different styles: an emotional ballad, an Italian opera section, and heavy rock.
>
> The band spent weeks layering their voices to create the opera section. Today, it has over two billion streams on Spotify and is considered one of the greatest pieces of music ever recorded.
>
> Let's take a quick look at this clip to feel the vibe of that song!"
>
*(▶️ Dica: Você pode dar play no trailer/clipe por 20 a 30 segundos, ou apenas apontar para o frame e continuar).*

## 🟡 Slide 4: Live Aid 1985 (~1m45s)
**Visual:** Stats + Live Aid video frame.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Ten years later, in July 1985, Queen gave the performance of their lives at **Live Aid**. 
>
> Live Aid was a massive charity concert at Wembley Stadium in London to raise money for famine relief in Africa. It was broadcast live on television to nearly two billion people worldwide.
>
> Queen only had **20 minutes** on stage. Other huge stars were there, like Elton John, David Bowie, and U2. But Queen completely stole the show.
>
> Freddie Mercury walked onto the stage and had total control over 72,000 people. He did the famous vocal warmup—the *'Ay-Oh!'* call-and-response—and the entire stadium sang back in unison.
>
> To this day, major music critics and magazines consider those 20 minutes the single greatest live rock performance in history."

---

## 🟡 Slide 5: Iconic Albums & Diversity (~1m45s)
**Visual:** 4 album covers.

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Another thing I really admire about Queen is their musical courage. They never stayed comfortable in just one genre. 
>
> If you look at their most famous albums:
> - In **A Night at the Opera (1975)**, they explored rock opera and acoustic ballads like *Love of My Life*.
> - In **News of the World (1977)**, they wanted songs where the audience could participate with their feet and hands. That’s how *We Will Rock You* and *We Are The Champions* were born—now the biggest sports anthems on the planet.
> - In **Jazz (1978)**, they released *Don’t Stop Me Now*, which scientists recently proved to be one of the happiest songs ever written because of its tempo and positive chords.
> - And in **The Game (1980)**, they embraced funk and disco with *Another One Bites the Dust*, which became a huge dance club hit.
>
> They showed the world that a rock band can play anything they want."

---

## 🟡 Slide 6: The Legacy & Q&A (~1m15s)
**Visual:** Final quote + "Thank you".

### 🗣️ What to say:
> "Tragically, Freddie Mercury passed away in 1991, but his spirit and Queen’s music will truly live forever.
>
> As Freddie used to say: *'The show must go on.'*
>
> For me personally, Queen represents freedom, creativity, and the courage to be yourself without caring what other people think. Their songs make you feel powerful, energetic, and happy whenever you put your headphones on.
>
> Thank you so much for your time and attention! 
>
> Now, I’d love to know: **what is your favorite Queen song, or which one do you remember the most?**"

## 🛡️ "Save-My-Life" Pocket Guide (Frases prontas se você travar)
- Se esquecer a palavra: *"Let me rephrase that..."* ou *"How can I explain this... well, basically..."*
- Se quiser passar o slide: *"Moving on to our next topic..."* ou *"Now, let's take a look at..."*
- Se alguém fizer uma pergunta e você não entender: *"Could you repeat that a bit slower, please?"*
